# EchoCircles — Privacy Policy

*Effective Date: October 23, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how **EchoCircles** ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Credentials:** When you register an account, we collect your username, email address, and encrypted/hashed password.
- **User-Generated Content:** We collect the posts, discussions, replies, status updates, and formatted text that you publish to topic circles and the feed.
- **Device Identifiers & Advertising Data:** Our integration with Google AdMob collects advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) and crash logs to serve non-intrusive banner advertisements and maintain stability.
- **AI Assistant Conversations:** Messages sent directly to the built-in AI assistant (Lilly) are processed securely to provide instant responses and recommendations.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To authenticate and maintain your user account.
- To display real-time community feeds, discussions, and member online presence.
- To serve banner advertisements via Google Mobile Ads.
- To protect the community against spam and policy violations.

---

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers
We do not sell personal data. We only use reputable infrastructure providers:
- **Supabase Inc.:** Database hosting, authentication, and real-time sync.
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** In-app banner advertising.
- **Google Generative AI (Gemini):** AI guide Lilly responses.

---

## 4. Data Security
All network traffic between EchoCircles and backend servers uses **HTTPS (TLS 1.3)** and Secure WebSockets (WSS). Passwords are encrypted using strong BCrypt cryptographic hashing.

---

## 5. Your Rights
- [Delete Content](delete-content.md): Remove individual posts and comments at any time.
- [Delete Account](delete-account.md): Permanently purge your profile and all associated data.
