Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

Summary: EchoCircles respects your privacy. We collect minimal personal data required to operate your account and community discussions. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Introduction

Welcome to EchoCircles ("we", "our", or "us"), developed and published by MobileStudio (Developer: SupriyoPaulTM). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("EchoCircles" or the "App") and our official website hosted at https://supriyopaultm.github.io/echocircles/.

By downloading, accessing, or using EchoCircles, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, as well as information generated automatically through your use of the App:

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we may collect your username, display name, email address, password (cryptographically hashed using industry-standard algorithms), bio, avatar URL, and profile preferences.
  • User Content: Information and content you post, create, or share on the platform, including circle creations, discussion posts, articles, poll votes, quiz answers, comments, direct messages, and chat messages.
  • Engagement & Reputation Data: Experience points (EP), leaderboard standings, badges, upvotes, and circle membership statistics.
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: When you use the app, limited diagnostic data such as app version, device model, operating system version, crash reports, and performance metrics may be collected to identify bugs and improve stability.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We may integrate trusted third-party service providers to facilitate app operations and monetization:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): We display advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate IP location, and ad interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. You can manage your ad personalization settings through your device’s Google account settings or Android privacy controls.
  • Supabase Cloud Database: Our backend data storage and real-time syncing services are powered by Supabase. Your data is transmitted via secure HTTPS/TLS encryption and stored in managed cloud databases with Row-Level Security protections.
  • Google Play Services: Used for in-app updates, licensing, and core framework support.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for specific, legitimate purposes, including:

  • Providing, maintaining, and updating the EchoCircles application.
  • Enabling user authentication, profile personalization, and account management.
  • Facilitating circle communities, posts, live polls, trivia quizzes, and real-time messaging.
  • Calculating user progression, badges, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Protecting users against spam, harassment, abusive conduct, and security breaches.
  • Notifying you about available app updates and community activity.

5. Data Retention & Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational precautions to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All communication between the app and our database is conducted over encrypted TLS connections. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the application services, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our agreements.

6. Your Rights & Data Choices

You have control over your data within EchoCircles:

  • Access and Update: You can edit your profile details (display name, bio, avatar, privacy preferences) at any time through the in-app Profile Settings screen.
  • Account Deletion: You may request the deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us directly at supriopaul.all@gmail.com. Upon verification, your profile and private information will be permanently removed.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of interest-based advertising in your Android device settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID.

7. Children's Privacy

EchoCircles is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under these ages. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete such data immediately from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or relevant laws. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
