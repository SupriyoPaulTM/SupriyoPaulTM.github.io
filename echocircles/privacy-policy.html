EchoCircles — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EchoCircles ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: When you register an account, we collect your username, email address, and encrypted/hashed password.
  • User-Generated Content: We collect the posts, discussions, replies, status updates, and formatted text that you publish to topic circles and the feed.
  • Device Identifiers & Advertising Data: Our integration with Google AdMob collects advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and crash logs to serve non-intrusive banner advertisements and maintain service stability.
  • AI Assistant Conversations: Messages sent directly to the built-in AI assistant (Lilly) are processed securely to provide instant responses and recommendations.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and authenticate your user account.
  • To render real-time community feeds, discussions, and member online presence.
  • To personalize community circle interactions.
  • To serve banner advertisements via Google Mobile Ads.
  • To protect our community against spam, unauthorized access, and policy violations.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with reputable service providers necessary to operate the application:

  • Supabase Inc.: For secure database hosting, user authentication, and real-time synchronization.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): For delivering in-app advertising banners.
  • Google Generative AI (Gemini): For powering the automated conversational capabilities of AI guide Lilly.

4. Data Security

All network communications between the EchoCircles app and backend servers are strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS (TLS 1.3) and Secure WebSockets (WSS). Sensitive credentials such as passwords are encrypted using strong cryptographic hashing algorithms (BCrypt).

5. Your Rights: Access, Deletion & Correction

You have full ownership of your data. You may:

  • Delete Individual Content: Delete posts or replies at any time in-app or via our Content Deletion Instructions.
  • Permanently Delete Your Account: Submit an account deletion request in-app or via our Account Deletion Instructions.
