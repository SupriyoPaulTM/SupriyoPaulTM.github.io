# EchoCircles — Account & Data Deletion

This page provides official instructions for requesting permanent deletion of your **EchoCircles** user account and associated personal data, in compliance with Google Play Data Deletion Policies.

---

## 1. How to Request Account Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your EchoCircles account and associated data using either of the following methods:

### Method A: Web / Email Request (No App Re-installation Required)
1. Send an email to our support team at: **`supriyopaul.dev@gmail.com`**
2. Use the subject line: **`EchoCircles Account Deletion Request`**
3. In the body of your message, provide:
   - Your registered **Username**
   - Your registered **Email Address** (used during sign up)
4. Our team will verify your identity and permanently delete your account within **7 business days**.

### Method B: Direct In-App Request
1. Open the **EchoCircles** app on your device.
2. Sign into your account.
3. Navigate to **Profile / Settings**.
4. Select **Delete Account & Data** and confirm your request.

---

## 2. What Data is Permanently Deleted?

When your account deletion request is processed, the following information is permanently wiped from our database servers:
- **User Profile:** Username, display name, registered email address, hashed password credentials, and avatar.
- **User Activity:** All forum posts, discussion replies, status updates, and comments created by your account.
- **Community Memberships:** Your membership status, circle associations, and administrative roles.
- **Chat History:** Direct conversation history and stored AI companion sessions.

---

## 3. Data Retention Policy & Exceptions

- **Permanent Purge:** Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered.
- **Security Audit Logs:** Cryptographic server access logs may be retained for up to 30 days solely for security monitoring and fraud prevention, after which they are automatically rotated and purged.
- **Aggregated Statistics:** Fully anonymized metrics (e.g. cumulative total post counts without author linkage) that cannot be linked back to you.

---

## 4. Deleting Only Specific Content

If you wish to delete individual posts or comments without deleting your entire account, refer to the [Content Deletion Instructions](delete-content.md).
