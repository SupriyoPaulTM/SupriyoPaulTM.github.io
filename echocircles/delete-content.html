EchoCircles — Content & Post Deletion

Instructions for Deleting Individual Posts, Comments & Media

Granular Content Control: You do not need to delete your entire EchoCircles account to remove individual posts, comments, or discussions. You retain full control over content you author.

1. In-App Content Deletion (Immediate)

You can remove any post or reply you have published directly inside the app:

  1. Open the EchoCircles app and ensure you are signed in.
  2. Locate the post, thread, or reply you wish to remove (in your Feed, Circle Discussion, or from your Profile History).
  3. Tap the action menu button (⋮ or More Options) located on the upper-right corner of your post card.
  4. Select Delete Post (or Delete Comment).
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt. The content is immediately deleted from the live feed and removed from the database.

2. Content Removal via Web Support Request

If you cannot access the app or wish to request content removal via the web:

  1. Send an email to supriyopaul.dev@gmail.com.
  2. Subject: EchoCircles Content Removal Request.
  3. Include:
    • Your registered Username or Email
    • The Post ID or direct reference/link to the post
    • Brief reason for removal
  4. Our moderation team will review and permanently delete the specified content within 48 hours.

3. What Happens When Content is Deleted?

  • Live Feed: The item disappears immediately from all community feeds and search results.
  • Database: The record and any cached representations in local Room stores are permanently removed.
  • Replies & Threading: Child comments may either be cascade-deleted or displayed as [Post deleted by author] to maintain conversation context.

4. Looking to Delete Your Whole Account?

If you want to permanently delete your entire profile and all associated data, visit our Account & Data Deletion Instructions.
