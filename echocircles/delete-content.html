Content & Post Deletion Guide

Effective Date: October 23, 2026 | Google Play Data Safety Compliance

Granular Content Control: You do not need to delete your entire EchoCircles account to remove individual posts, comments, poll submissions, or discussions. You retain total control over any content you author.

1. Overview

EchoCircles allows users to delete specific pieces of user-generated content without closing their account or forfeiting their community badges, circle memberships, and progression.

If you are looking to permanently delete your entire profile and all associated personal data, please consult our Account Deletion Guide.

2. How to Delete Individual Posts (In-App Step-by-Step)

To delete any discussion post, article, or announcement you created:

  1. Open the App: Launch EchoCircles on your device and ensure you are signed in.
  2. Locate Your Post: Navigate to the post you wish to remove (found in the Home Feed, within the specific Circle, or under your Profile Post History).
  3. Open Post Menu: Tap the three-dot action icon (⋮ or More Options) located on the upper-right corner of your post card.
  4. Select Delete: Tap "Delete Post" from the dropdown menu.
  5. Confirm Deletion: Tap "Confirm" in the prompt.

Immediate Effect: The post is removed instantly from the live community feed, real-time subscriber views, and the central Supabase database.

3. How to Delete Comments & Replies (In-App Step-by-Step)

To remove a comment or reply you contributed to an existing discussion:

  1. Open the Discussion Thread: Tap on the parent post to view its discussion and comment stream.
  2. Find Your Comment: Scroll to the comment you wish to delete.
  3. Select Delete: Tap the options icon beside your comment and tap "Delete Comment".
  4. Confirm: Tap "Delete" to confirm. The comment will be removed immediately.

4. Content Removal via Web Support Request

If you are unable to access the app or require administrative assistance to remove content:

  1. Send an email to our developer support:
    supriopaul.all@gmail.com
  2. Use Subject Line:
    EchoCircles Content Removal Request
  3. In the email body, please include:
    • Your registered Username or registered Email address
    • The Post ID, direct title, or description of the content to be deleted
    • A screenshot or timestamp of the post (if available)
  4. Our team will review ownership and permanently remove the content within 48 hours.

5. What Happens to Threaded Replies?

When a root post with replies is deleted by its author, EchoCircles automatically updates the thread so that any replies either cascade-delete or clearly display as [Post deleted by author] to preserve community context while completely wiping the original text and author identity.
