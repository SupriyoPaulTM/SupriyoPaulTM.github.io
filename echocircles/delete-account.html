Account & Data Deletion Guide

Effective Date: October 23, 2026 | Google Play Data Safety Compliance

Full User Ownership: In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy standards, EchoCircles guarantees that you can request the permanent deletion of your user account and all associated personal data at any time.

1. Overview

When you create an account on EchoCircles, you retain full ownership and control of your identity. You may request permanent deletion of your account and personal data either directly through the mobile application or via our web-based email support pathway without needing to reinstall the app.

If you only wish to delete specific posts, comments, or discussions without deleting your entire account, please visit our Content Deletion Guide.

2. How to Delete Your Account (In-App Step-by-Step)

To delete your account directly inside the Android application:

  1. Open the App: Launch EchoCircles on your Android device and sign into your account.
  2. Open Profile / Settings: Tap your Avatar in the top app bar or open the side navigation drawer and select Settings.
  3. Navigate to Account Settings: Select Account & Privacy.
  4. Select Delete Account: Scroll down to the bottom "Danger Zone" section and tap "Request Account Deletion".
  5. Confirm Deletion: Read the confirmation summary and enter your current password to verify ownership. Tap "Permanently Delete My Account".

Once confirmed, your authentication session is immediately terminated, you will be logged out, and your account deletion request will be processed.

3. How to Request Deletion via Web / Email (No App Required)

If you have already uninstalled the application, lost access to your mobile device, or prefer submitting your request online, you can submit an account deletion request via email:

  1. Send an email to our official developer support address:
    supriopaul.all@gmail.com
  2. Use the exact Subject Line:
    EchoCircles Account Deletion Request
  3. In the body of your message, please provide:
    • Your registered Username (e.g., @yourusername)
    • The Email Address registered with your EchoCircles account
  4. Verification & Processing: To protect account security, we will send a brief verification email to your registered address. Upon confirmation, your account and all associated data will be permanently wiped from our database within 7 business days.

4. What Data is Permanently Deleted?

Upon successful execution of an account deletion request, the following information is permanently and irreversibly purged from our Supabase cloud database:

  • Authentication & Profile Data: User ID, username, display name, registered email address, hashed password, avatar image, and bio.
  • User-Generated Content: All posts, articles, poll votes, trivia quiz submissions, and comments created by your account.
  • Community Memberships: Your membership records, circle roles, and permissions across all circles.
  • Progression & Rewards: Experience points (EP), earned badges, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Direct Conversations: Private messages and stored conversation sessions with the AI guide (Lilly).

5. Data Retention & Security Policy

EchoCircles does not retain personal data after an account is deleted. However, in accordance with industry security best practices:

  • Security Audit Logs: Standard cryptographic server access logs (such as IP addresses and request timestamps) may be held in rolling server memory for up to 30 days solely for DDoS mitigation, abuse prevention, and legal compliance, after which they are permanently overwritten.
  • Anonymized Metrics: Aggregate platform counts (e.g., cumulative historical platform statistics) that are completely detached from your identity may remain without any personal linkage.
