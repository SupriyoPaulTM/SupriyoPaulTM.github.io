EchoCircles — Account & Data Deletion

Official Account and User Data Deletion Instructions

User Control & Transparency: You have the complete right to request the deletion of your EchoCircles account and any personal data or content associated with it at any time.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your EchoCircles account and associated data using either of the following methods:

Method A: Web / Email Request (No App Re-installation Required)

  1. Send an email to our support team at: supriyopaul.dev@gmail.com (or contact developer via GitHub).
  2. Use the subject line: EchoCircles Account Deletion Request.
  3. In the body of your message, please provide:
    • Your registered Username
    • Your registered Email Address (used during sign up)
  4. Our team will confirm your identity and permanently delete your account within 7 business days.

Method B: Direct In-App Request

  1. Open the EchoCircles app on your device.
  2. Sign into your account.
  3. Navigate to Profile / Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account & Data and confirm your request.

2. What Data is Permanently Deleted?

When your account deletion request is processed, the following information is permanently and irrevocably wiped from our Supabase servers:

  • User Profile: Username, display name, registered email address, hashed password credentials, and avatar.
  • User Activity: All forum posts, discussion replies, status updates, and comments created by your account.
  • Community Memberships: Your membership status, circle associations, and administrative roles.
  • Chat History: Direct conversation history and stored AI companion sessions.

3. Data Retention Policy & Exceptions

Permanent Purge: Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered. Your username will be released.

We do not retain personal identifiable information once deleted. However, minimal non-personal records may be kept temporarily:

  • Security and Fraud Audit Logs: Cryptographic server access logs may be retained for up to 30 days solely for security monitoring, after which they are automatically rotated and purged.
  • Aggregated Statistics: Fully anonymized metrics (e.g. cumulative total post counts without author linkage) that cannot be linked back to you.

4. How to Delete Only Specific Content

If you wish to delete individual posts or comments without deleting your entire account, visit our Content Deletion Instructions.
